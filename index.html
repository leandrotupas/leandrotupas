03 / HOW I THINK THROUGH DESIGN

Good work starts
with a good question.

From a first conversation to the last tiny adjustment, I want every design decision to have a reason behind it.

01 ↗

Understand
the why.

Start with the story, the audience, the context and the problem. The brief is a starting point, not the entire answer.

02 ↗

Find the
visual idea.

Explore directions, sharpen the concept and build a visual language that has something to say.

03 ↗

Make it
work everywhere.

Carry the idea through typography, layouts, applications and guidelines — so it stays recognizable beyond the logo.

I'm Yancy, a graphic and brand designer based in Davao City, Philippines. I've spent over five years helping brands find their voice through identities, campaigns, typography, visual systems and lots of collaboration.

With a background in Computer Science and a love for visual storytelling, I move between big-picture thinking and the small details that make something work. I want the work to have purpose, personality, and room to grow.
