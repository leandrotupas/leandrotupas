# Flagship Case Studies — Storytelling Questionnaire

Answer one project at a time. Brief notes are fine. Please leave missing information blank rather than guessing.

## 01. Advenient — completed in Version 16

- Led client communication and brand design.
- Considered gradients, other colorways and an alternative logo direction that did not resonate.
- The client wanted teal and orange; the main mark used an exponent-like caret (^) to express growth.
- Around two to three rounds of design revisions per deliverable; the final work was approved after revisions.
- Real-world applications included cards, employee IDs and ID slings.

## 02. KOTA Center Davao — completed in Version 17

- Created the logo, brand colors, typography, social style guide, and adaptable layouts for Facebook covers, Facebook event covers, square posts, and Instagram Stories.
- The client asked to retain red, represent KOTA respectfully, and avoid visual directions that pandered to Davao culture.
- The client offered a book for inspiration (remembered as possibly about saints); a Grand Cross motif in it suggested a K-like shape, which later crystallized in a dream.
- Rejected appropriating Indigenous motifs of Davao as superficial branding decoration; pivoted to a distinct K-shaped identity.
- The client was happy that KOTA finally had its own visual identity. Based on the designer's observation, the branding continued to be used on the organization's Facebook page and has evolved under later contributors. No performance metrics claimed.

## 03. Wünderbar — completed in Version 18

- Hired to create the Wünderbar identity. The designer executed the brand design and worked directly with the client on creative direction.
- Prepared exactly 21 logo variations so the client could compare possibilities.
- Chose Gothic-inspired typography for the European-inspired drink concept, and a cocktail-stirring spoon as a relevant symbol. The navy-and-gold visual direction was requested by the client.
- Client and designer agreed on the same preferred logo; client liked it and there were no major pushbacks to resolve.
- Designed Facebook covers, social posts, business cards, signage, and menus. The **cocktail illustrations in the menus were made by another creative**.
- Client has kept the identity in use and expressed gratitude. Client reported expanding into flea markets and weddings. Social content has evolved with later contributors; no business growth is attributed to the identity without evidence.
